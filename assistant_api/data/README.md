# Legal corpus

Scope: Russian independent guarantees, with a focus on procurement regulation.

## Sources

- `GK_368_379.txt` — Civil Code of the Russian Federation, Articles 368–379 (§ 6, independent guarantee).
- `44FZ_article_45.txt` — Federal Law No. 44-FZ, Article 45.
- `223FZ_article_3_4_guarantees.txt` — guarantee-related provisions of Article 3.4 of Federal Law No. 223-FZ.
- `PP_1005.txt` — Government Resolution No. 1005 of 08.11.2013: requirements, registers, payment-demand form and standard guarantee forms for 44-FZ.
- `PP_1397.txt` — Government Resolution No. 1397 of 09.08.2022: requirements, forms and register rules for independent guarantees in SME procurements under 223-FZ.
- `VS_independent_guarantee_2019.txt` — Supreme Court Review approved 05.06.2019; all 17 legal positions.
- `VS_contract_system_2017_guarantees.txt` — Supreme Court procurement review approved 28.06.2017; introductory framework plus positions 25 and 30 relevant to guarantees.

## Corpus policy

The files contain source legal text rather than paraphrases. Editorial amendment-history notes and unrelated amendment blocks were removed to reduce retrieval noise. Source structure, numbered provisions, legal-position numbers and standard-form sections are preserved.

The application should identify the applicable source layer rather than mechanically merge all sources:

1. Civil Code — general independent-guarantee rules.
2. 44-FZ / 223-FZ — special procurement rules.
3. Government Resolutions No. 1005 / No. 1397 — implementing requirements and standard forms.
4. Supreme Court reviews — interpretation and application.

Article 369 of the Civil Code is kept only as a single historical-status line (it lost force on 1 June 2015 under Federal Law No. 42-FZ); no repealed substantive wording is included.

Historical use of the term "bank guarantee" in court materials remains unchanged in source text and is treated as part of the independent-guarantee subject area.
